About Malazo Realtors

Your first choice real estate partner in Kenya

Since our founding, we've helped hundreds of families and investors find their perfect property — from prime Nairobi plots to coastal retreats.

Built on trust, driven by results

Malazo Realtors was founded by Christine Eve and Aleesha Brown with a clear mission: make property ownership in Kenya accessible, transparent, and rewarding.

Based in Hurlingham, Nairobi, we specialise in residential and commercial properties across Kenya — from land in Malindi to premium plots in Woodley and beyond. Every transaction is guided by one principle: your investment should feel as secure as it is exciting.

We don't just list properties. We walk the ground, verify every title, and stand behind every deal. That's why our clients come back — and bring their friends.
