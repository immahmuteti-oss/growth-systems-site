Sir Francis Homes

The challenge

A Nairobi real-estate firm with hundreds of listings needed buyers to enquire, and needed to know which ads actually produced those enquiries.

What I did

  • Landing pages for individual developments (A-One Pandora, Golfscape): price and deposit terms up front, an enquiry form that captures budget and timeline, and a WhatsApp button.
  • Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking, so every website enquiry is tied to the ad that produced it.
  • Search fixes across all 249 listings (structured data Google reads), plus speed, caching and layout repairs on the live site.

Where it stands

The pages and fixes are live and the site runs on them. Dismas, who led the project on the client side, is a reference.

FemGyn Healthcare Clinic, Ruiru

The challenge

A women's health clinic needed a professional home online, where patients could find services, get answers and book without friction.

What I did

  • Designed, built and deployed the whole website on the clinic's own hosting: services, patient tools, and WhatsApp and booking on every page.
  • Built a WhatsApp assistant and a staff-only front desk for enquiries, bookings and emergency escalation to the doctor and nurse.
  • Set up Google Search Console; the site is indexed.

Where it stands

Live and serving patients in Ruiru. Dr. Chris is a reference.
