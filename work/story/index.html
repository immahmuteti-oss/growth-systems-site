My story

I didn't start with clients. I started with a decision.

How a student of security and forensics became a builder of systems that businesses rely on, and what the hard months taught me.

My name is Emmanuel Muteti. I study Information Security & Forensics at KCA University. My training is in finding how systems fail, and that's exactly what made me want to build ones that don't.

Before I had a single client, I built. A patient concierge for a hospital. A lead engine that follows up on its own. A security-operations system for guard companies. A 3D city in the browser. Nobody paid for those. I built them because I wanted to know I could, and because I wanted every future client to see a working system, not a promise.

As AI-fficiency, I took that work into the Medical Innovation & Accelerator Hub at KUTRRH, Kenyatta University Teaching, Referral & Research Hospital, working alongside other innovators on AI for patient services and hospital operations.

In May, I went out to find my first clients. Nobody tells you how much of the work happens before anyone says yes.

The months since have been the real classroom. There were long nights, delivering more than was asked because I'd rather over-deliver than explain. There were people who wanted the work without the trust, and conversations where I learned, sometimes the hard way, that not everyone you build for shares your intentions.

I kept going, and I kept sharpening the craft. I took on problems outside my job description, from search fixes across hundreds of property listings to the security settings that stop fraudsters from sending email in a firm's name, and stayed until the numbers were true.

That's what I want to be known for: honest work that keeps working after I've left the room. And it's why, today, I'm deliberate about who I work with. I'm looking for clients I can grow with, people who care about their customers as much as I care about the craft.
